# Documented Policy Decisions

Inspect the task-relevant policy and implementation. Verify changeable external
terms from primary sources; record source URLs and dates where the decision
depends on them. Keep technical decisions distinct from qualified legal or
human release approval.

Update the canonical document and link it instead of repeating policy across
instructions. Run checks for the affected docs or policy. For material policy
changes, supply acceptance criteria, relevant files/diff, and verification to
the repository's fresh Codex review. Fix blockers and keep optional follow-up
separate. Tracker synchronization and external-model audit packets apply only
when explicitly requested.
